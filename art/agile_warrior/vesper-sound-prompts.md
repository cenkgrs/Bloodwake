# Vesper ses efektleri — ElevenLabs Sound Effects prompt'ları

Her ses için süreyi ElevenLabs'te ayarla ("Duration"). "Darbe anı", oyunda hasarın
vurduğu an: ses o noktada zirve yapmalı. Dosyaları aynı adla
`unity/Assets/Bloodwake/Resources/audio/` içine koy; `rules.json` ve darbe zamanlamasını ben bağlarım.

Ortak karakter: ağır büyük kılıç (greatsword), siyah-altın alev, kan. Gerçekçi, kuru
(fazla yankısız), oyun içi kısa SFX; müzik yok, ses yok (vocal yok).

## Prompt'lar

**vesper_attack_1.wav**
> Heavy greatsword horizontal slash, deep whoosh of a large blade cutting air, a crackle of fire trailing the swing, armored leather creak, short and punchy, no impact, dry game SFX

**vesper_attack_2.wav**
> Heavy greatsword backhand diagonal slash, slightly higher-pitched whoosh than the first swing, flame roaring along the blade, metal armor rattle, short and punchy, no impact, dry game SFX

**vesper_attack_3_thrust.wav**
> Powerful upward greatsword thrust finisher, sharp rising blade swish, sudden burst of fire whooshing forward, heavy armored step and grunt-free exertion, ends on a hot flame flare, dry game SFX

**vesper_hit_flesh.wav** (3 varyasyon)
> Greatsword striking armored flesh, heavy meaty impact with a metallic clang underneath, small sizzle of fire on contact, short, dry game SFX

**vesper_dash.wav**
> Fast dash burst, a whoosh of flame and ember smoke, crackling sparks scattering, cloth and armor flutter, very short, dry game SFX

**vesper_ember_dash.wav**
> Quick forward dash through fire followed by an upward greatsword thrust, rushing flame whoosh, then a sharp blade swish and a fiery burst on the thrust, dry game SFX

**vesper_cinder_ward.wav**
> Greatsword driven down into stone with a resonant metallic ring, then a tight eruption of black-and-gold flame bursting up around it, crackling embers falling, dry game SFX

**vesper_bloodfire_rite.wav**
> Massive greatsword slam into the ground, a deep cathedral bell toll rings out, followed by a wide roaring eruption of blood and fire spreading outward, low rumble and crackling embers tail, epic dark fantasy, dry game SFX

**vesper_death.wav**
> Armored knight collapsing, greatsword clattering onto stone, a last dying flame sputtering out with fading embers, heavy body fall, dark and somber, dry game SFX
