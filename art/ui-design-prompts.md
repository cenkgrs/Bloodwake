# Bloodwake UI tasarım prompt'ları

Önce ekran mockup'larını üret (1–4), birini beğenince aynı stilde UI kit'i (5) üret.
Kit'ten parçaları kesip oyuna 9-slice olarak bağlayacağım; mockup'lar yerleşim referansı.

Ortak stil (her prompt'un sonuna ekle):
> dark gothic fantasy action RPG UI, inspired by Diablo IV and Path of Exile 2 menus, restrained
> and elegant, thin forged-iron and aged-silver filigree, deep oxblood red accents, black lacquer
> panels with subtle leather and stone texture, crisp serif typography (Cinzel / Trajan style),
> generous negative space, high resolution 16:9 game screenshot, 4K, sharp, no blur, no watermark

**1. Ana menü**
> Main menu screen of a dark fantasy game called BLOODWAKE: large engraved title logo top-left
> with a faint blood-red glow, a moody painted night scene of a cursed gothic town with fog and a
> blood moon filling the background, a narrow vertical menu on the left with text-only options
> PLAY, SETTINGS, EXIT separated by thin silver dividers, the hovered option marked by a small
> glowing red sigil, tiny version text bottom-right

**2. Sınıf seçimi**
> Character selection screen: the chosen hero rendered large on the left in dramatic rim light,
> on the right a tall lacquered panel with the class name in large engraved serif, a short
> italic subtitle, a paragraph of lore, four thin stat bars with small icons, three ability icons
> in circular filigree frames with key labels Q E SPACE and short descriptions, a row of four small
> hero portrait tabs along the top, a primary "BEGIN THE HUNT" button bottom-right

**3. Oyun içi HUD**
> In-game HUD of an isometric dark fantasy ARPG: top-left a compact health globe or bar with an
> ornate silver frame and a small character portrait, bottom-center three square ability slots
> with cooldown sweep overlay and key labels, minimal and readable, does not cover the action

**4. NPC diyalog**
> Dialogue screen: an NPC bust portrait on the left fading into darkness, a wide lacquered dialogue
> box along the bottom with the speaker name in engraved gold serif, lore text in readable serif,
> small "continue" and "close" prompts, a subtle vignette over the game world behind

**5. UI kit (asıl kullanılacak parçalar)**
> Game UI kit sprite sheet on a flat neutral grey background, in the exact style above: one large
> empty panel frame, one small empty panel frame, three button states (normal, hover, pressed)
> as long horizontal empty plaques, one square ability slot frame, one circular icon frame, a
> horizontal health bar frame (empty), a thin ornamental divider, a small corner ornament; every
> element separated with space, straight-on orthographic view, no text, high resolution 4K
